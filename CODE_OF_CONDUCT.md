# Code of Conduct

This is a small packaging repository. The expectations are not complicated.

## Expected

- Be civil. Disagree about the technical question, not the person.
- Assume the other person is trying to help. Most bug reports that read as rude
  are just someone frustrated and short on time.
- When reporting a problem, include what was asked for in the template. It is
  the difference between a fix and a week of back-and-forth.
- When answering, remember the other person cannot see your machine.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately wasting maintainer time: spam, bad-faith reports, or repeatedly
  ignoring the issue template.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and commit messages in this
repository.

## Enforcement

Report problems through a
[private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) (it works for
conduct reports too and is not public) or by contacting the repository owner
directly.

Reports are handled privately. Responses range from a request to change
behaviour, through comment removal, to blocking from the repository, depending
on severity. Whoever reports something is not obliged to justify themselves
publicly.

If the person you need to report is the maintainer, GitHub's own
[reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
exists for that.
